# Yingjie Tan

I am an AI researcher interested in how large language models use context, represent knowledge, route information, and make decisions.

## Research, documented in the open

My current work focuses on mechanistic interpretability, sparse attention, long-context inference, context compression, and deep-search agents.

I use this site for research notes, experiment reports, paper discussions, engineering write-ups, and ideas that are still taking shape. The goal is to make the reasoning—not only the final result—clear enough to inspect.

“A useful research note should preserve the question, the evidence, and the uncertainty between them.”

### Research interests

- Mechanistic interpretability
- DSA and sparse attention
- Long-context reasoning
- Information flow in language models
- Context and KV-cache compression
- Efficient inference
- Deep-search agents
