# Personal Profile

I am a Ph.D. student at Tsinghua University. My research interests lie in understanding machine learning models and neural networks. Starting from observed phenomena, I analyze the root causes of model behavior and aim to develop practical learning methods that are efficient and generalizable. Recently, I have focused on context compression for DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) architectures and on training stability. You can read my [personal blog](blog.llms.md).
