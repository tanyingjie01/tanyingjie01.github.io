### [From a Model’s Random-Number Fingerprint:\
A Study of LLM Reasoning Circuits](posts/random-number-fingerprint-circuit/index.llms.md)

Starting from a model fingerprint that consistently emerges in random-number generation, this post identifies a class of heads that transport candidate information. When an answer has not been effectively inferred and the model must still choose among multiple candidates, these heads produce significant causal effects. Their role generalizes across candidates, tasks, and architectures, and is consistently reproduced in various intervention experiments that interrupt normal reasoning circuits. We further use these heads’ TV values as a signal for detecting unreliable reasoning and improve the model’s reasoning performance.
